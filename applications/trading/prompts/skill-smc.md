# Skill: SMC Patterns

How to read the `structure` block of the snapshot.

- **Trend and events.** A `break_of_structure` continues the trend; a `change_of_character` is the first break against it and is the earliest sign of a turn. `candles_ago` says how fresh each is: an event from 30 candles back is history.
- **Range and zone.** `range_low`..`range_high` is the current dealing range. "discount" is its lower part, "premium" its upper part. Buying in discount and selling in premium gives room to the target; buying deep in premium leaves little.
- **Order blocks.** An unmitigated bullish block below price is where buyers stepped in: a logical place for a long's stop to sit just under. A bearish block above price is the same for shorts.
- **Fair value gaps.** An unfilled gap is a zone price often returns to. A gap between price and the target is a reason to bring the target closer.
- **Liquidity sweeps.** A bullish sweep (lows taken, close back above) removes the sell stops under a low and often precedes a move up; a bearish sweep mirrors it.

These are mechanical labels on past candles, not a promise. Weigh them with the indicators.
