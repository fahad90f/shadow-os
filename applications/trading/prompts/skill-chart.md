# Skill: Chart Reading

You also receive a picture of the chart: candles (green up, red down), EMA 20 (yellow), EMA 50 (violet), Bollinger bands (blue), volume bars at the bottom, newest candle on the right.

- Describe the shape to yourself first: trending in a channel, ranging between two levels, or compressing into a point.
- Look at the last ten candles: long wicks rejecting a level, bodies getting smaller, or closing strongly in one direction.
- Check the picture against the numbers in the snapshot. If they disagree (the numbers say bullish, the picture shows a rounded top), say so in the reasoning and lower the confidence.
- Do not read anything into the picture that is not drawn there.
